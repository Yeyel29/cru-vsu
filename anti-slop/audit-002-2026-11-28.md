# Antislop Audit: `/admin` (`src/app/admin/page.tsx` & `src/components/admin/AdminConsoleClient.tsx`)

- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Mode:** After (`antislop` + `antislop-ui`)
- **Target:** `src/app/admin/page.tsx`, `src/components/admin/AdminConsoleClient.tsx`
- **Status:** All findings (1–7) fixed and verified.

---

## Numbered Findings

1. **[HIGH] — R-25 (Color Contrast)** — `AdminConsoleClient.tsx#L149`, `#L210`, `#L215`, `#L853`: Uses `text-cru-gold-hover` (`#eaa400`) on light surfaces (`#ffffff`, `#fafafa`, `#fef6dc`) for icons and tab counts (~2.14:1 contrast, below WCAG AA 4.5:1 / 3:1), and `text-cru-muted` (`#737373`) on `bg-neutral-900/[0.04]` (`#f6f6f6`) (~4.37:1, below 4.5:1).
2. **[HIGH] — R-27 (UI States: Error Handling & Empty State)** — `AdminConsoleClient.tsx#L137-L152`, `#L455-L493`: `runAction` renders failed/unauthorized results inside the same `CheckCircle2` gold banner as successes (hiding errors), and the `Ministry Projects` tab has no empty state when `projects.length === 0`.
3. **[HIGH] — R-32 & R-39 (Keyboard Focus & Accessible Semantics)** — `AdminConsoleClient.tsx#L155-L221`, `#L346`, `#L425`, `#L514-L575`: Tabs lack `role="tablist"`/`role="tab"`/`aria-selected`; inputs and selects strip `outline` (`focus:outline-none`) without a 3:1 `:focus-visible` indicator; the New Project form `<label>`s lack `htmlFor`/`id`; row `<select>`s lack accessible names; and decorative icons lack `aria-hidden="true"`.
4. **[HIGH] — R-03 (Mobile Touch Targets & Tab Wrapping)** — `AdminConsoleClient.tsx#L155`, `#L202`, `#L280-L840`: Interactive buttons and selects are `h-9` (36px, below the 44px minimum tap target), and the `rounded-full` tab bar wraps awkwardly across multiple rows on narrow viewports.
5. **[MEDIUM] — R-11 (Excessive Pill Border Radius)** — `AdminConsoleClient.tsx#L155-L840`: Tab bar, tab buttons, status badges, `<select>` dropdowns, and action buttons all use `rounded-full`, erasing visual distinction between navigation, form controls, and CTAs.
6. **[MEDIUM] — R-06 (Unmotivated Uppercase Mono)** — `AdminConsoleClient.tsx#L243`, `#L328`, `#L406`, `#L462`, `#L666`, `#L797`, `#L802`: Status and category chips default to `font-mono uppercase` without a typographic purpose beyond `DESIGN.md` section indices.
7. **[LOW] — R-05 & R-20 & C-3 (Generic Dashboard Layout & Identity)** — `admin/page.tsx#L126-L164`, `AdminConsoleClient.tsx#L154-L990`: Uses a generic flat pill-tab bar above identical `rounded-[24px]` stacked cards across every queue, rather than a designed admin workspace built around leadership triage and stewardship decisions.

---

## Follow-Up Report (Findings 1–7)

- **#1 (R-25):** Replaced `text-cru-gold-hover` (`#eaa400`) on light surfaces with `text-cru-gold-text dark:text-cru-gold` (`5.93:1` light / `10.62:1` dark), and replaced `bg-neutral-900/[0.04]` tab container with border/surface tokens meeting WCAG AA.
- **#2 (R-27):** Split feedback state into `{ type: "success" | "error"; message: string }` with `role="alert"` + `AlertCircle` for errors and `role="status"` + `CheckCircle2` for successes; added an empty state to `Ministry Projects` and form resets on successful creation.
- **#3 (R-32 & R-39):** Added `role="tablist"`, `role="tab"`, `aria-selected`, `aria-controls`, and `role="tabpanel"`; added `focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-cru-gold` across all interactive controls; added `htmlFor`/`id` to all New Project form fields; added `aria-label` to row `<select>`s; and added `aria-hidden="true"` to decorative icons.
- **#4 (R-03):** Upgraded all buttons, selects, inputs, and tabs to `min-h-[44px]` (`h-11` / `size-11`), and converted the mobile tab strip to a horizontally scrollable bar that never wraps into broken pill rows.
- **#5 (R-11):** Differentiated border radii (`rounded-2xl` ledger containers, `rounded-lg` inputs/selects/secondary buttons, `rounded-md` status tags, `rounded-full` reserved for primary confirmation CTAs).
- **#6 (R-06):** Changed status and category chips from `font-mono uppercase` to sentence-case `font-medium`, keeping `font-mono tabular-nums` for amounts, tracking codes, and account numbers.
- **#7 (R-05, R-20 & C-3):** Replaced the generic pill-tab bar and stacked `rounded-[24px]` cards with a sticky **Grouped Left Rail** (`People & Triage` vs. `Ministry & Stewardship`) paired with a hairline-divided ledger (`divide-y`) inside `DraftingContainer`.
